Two minutes, free, and it really helps

Ask your library to carry it

Public libraries buy books their patrons ask for. One purchase suggestion from you can put this book on the shelf for every student in your town who is wondering whether engineering is right for them.

You’ll need: your library card number · the details below

Copy the book details

Library forms ask for these. The ISBN matters most: it tells the library exactly which edition to order from its supplier.

Title: Should I Be an Engineer? Everything You Need to Choose Your Engineering Path
Author: James R. Martin, Jr.
ISBN: 9798996329137 (paperback, 7 x 10)
Publisher: V3 Management LLC, 2026
Format / price: Paperback, $19.99
Available from: Ingram (and other library vendors)

Tip: on a phone, press and hold on the box to copy it. If the form has one ISBN box, use 9798996329137.

Open your library’s “Suggest a Purchase” form

Most libraries call it “Suggest a Purchase,” “Request a Purchase” or “Recommend a Title.” Here are the forms for libraries around New Orleans and Baton Rouge:

Anywhere else: search for “[your library’s name] suggest a purchase.” It is often in the online catalog or under “My Account.” No form? Hand the details from Step 1 to anyone at the front desk. That works too.

Add one sentence about why

Most forms have a comments box. A short, honest reason helps the librarian decide. For example:

A career guide for high school and college students deciding whether engineering is right for them. It compares 24 engineering careers and includes a guide for parents and counselors. The author is a licensed engineer who has taught engineering at the University of New Orleans for twenty years.

Please request it only at libraries where you have a card. One real request from a local patron carries more weight than many from out of town.
