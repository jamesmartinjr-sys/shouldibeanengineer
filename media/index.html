Full

Jim Martin, PhD, PE, is a licensed professional engineer and president of Design Engineering, Inc., a civil engineering firm in Metairie, Louisiana, that works on bridges, drainage, roadways, levees and pump stations. He started as a drafter and rodman, has taught engineering at the University of New Orleans for twenty years, and is a Scoutmaster. He chose engineering in high school without ever asking what engineers do every day, and wrote Should I Be an Engineer? Everything You Need to Choose Your Engineering Path so students can compare 24 engineering careers side by side before they commit to a major. He speaks to high school students about engineering careers in person around New Orleans and by video anywhere.

About the book

A full-color guide to 24 engineering careers in five families: Civil, Mechanical, Electrical, Chemical, and Software & Computer. Every career gets the same layout, so a student can compare the work, the pay, the setting and an hour-by-hour “Day in the Life.”
